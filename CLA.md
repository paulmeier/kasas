# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Thank you for contributing to kasas!

Before your pull request can be merged, you must agree to this Contributor
License Agreement. This is a one-time requirement.

## Why a CLA?

kasas is currently MIT licensed. A CLA gives the project maintainer the legal
right to relicense the project in the future (for example, to a dual
commercial/open-source model) without needing to contact every contributor.
**Your contribution remains MIT licensed and your rights are not diminished.**
This only grants additional rights to the project maintainer.

## How to sign

When opening a pull request, the CLA Assistant bot will prompt you
automatically. You sign by leaving a comment on the PR:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

CLA signatures are recorded automatically via
[CLA Assistant](https://cla-assistant.io).
